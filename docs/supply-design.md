# Supply design and research

The owner requested randomly spawned powerups/items that support co-op without obscuring combat or making chassis interchangeable. An official reference, [Halo Infinite shield mechanics](https://support.halowaypoint.com/hc/en-us/articles/24284987877524-Shields-in-Halo-Infinite), informed the value of readable, temporary combat benefits. KittyMech's pickup rules and art are original and do not reproduce Halo assets or exact equipment rules.

Six caches per room begin with two each of repair, energy and coolant. Location/order varies by room, with admitted ground placement, objective/spawn spacing and no obstruction. Each grants a bounded immediate benefit: 25% maximum armor, 45 energy, or 65 heat removal plus missile readiness. Repair shares with visible co-op allies within 10 m. Grounded E interaction within 3 m and real line of sight avoid accidental pickups while passing or climbing. Full/blocked guidance leaves the cache for another pilot. One authoritative claim/event retires the item; after 55–75 seconds it returns with a new valid kind/location. No random invincibility, permanent stat gain or hidden damage multiplier is introduced.

Repair's mint hexagon, energy's cyan tall cell and coolant's amber compact canister remain distinguishable through shape/icon as well as color. Three Higgsfield-generated faceplates are local 256 px materials, with originals, prompts, job IDs, dimensions, hashes and reported provider parameters retained in the art ledger. Normal play needs no subscription or generation request.

Authority regressions cover simultaneous claims, genuinely beneficial use, co-op/rival sharing, static LOS, safe randomized placement, cooldown/relocation and immutable late-join snapshots. Browser/network captures separately validate ordinary E input and actual replication. Future mission-specific loot, salvage spending and temporary modifiers should receive the same bounded authority and readability checks.
