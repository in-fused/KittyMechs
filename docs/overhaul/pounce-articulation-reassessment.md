## The one v4 derivative design

Keep Pounce's panther skull/muzzle, narrow optics, folded ears, pilot/canopy, broad scapular mass, flank lamellae, turbines, dorsal copper rails, curved limb plates, four armored claw digits, dewclaws, hydraulic detail, and articulated tail. Keep all eight material families and consistent outward normals. Replace geometry only where a demonstrated articulation/support requirement demands it; preserve comparable detail density and a coherent feline silhouette. Do not make a smaller Pounce or replace the opponent with a scaled proxy.

**Actual joint spaces.** Split each shoulder mantle/thigh cowl into a fixed dorsal/flank armor arch and an upper-link moving cover. Both retain curved profiles and seams. Form a genuinely empty recessed socket under the arch around the existing joint, with a closed concave wall and visible mechanism inside. The parent solid must exclude the complete swept upper armor/structural envelope over the admitted fold and restraint corridor, plus **.015 source m (.012 native m) design clearance**. A Boolean cavity must be manifold and have correct interior-wall normals; deleting hidden faces or disabling collisions is not an equivalent implementation. Existing spherical bearing/collar pairs receive explicit rigid attachment ownership only for their unchanged intended mechanism volume. They do not clear nearby armor.

The two failed witnesses lie close to the joint rather than at the distal link. Their locations support beginning with socket radii on the order of .30–.33 source m, but those are construction estimates, **not proven clearance bounds**. The final cavity is the union of measured swept exterior volumes along the actual constrained path; a guessed sphere cannot replace that measurement. Stop if this removes the defining shoulder/hip silhouette or requires an unbounded cavity.

**Relieved link ends.** Replace the full closed upper loft at its proximal/distal caps with tapered armor ending outside each joint's swept exclusion volume. Initial construction setbacks are .20 source m at the upper proximal end and .15 m at its knee end; the remaining curved plate keeps its original width, longitudinal ribs and separate copper detail. Shin caps receive analogous measured relief, beginning at .18 m from the knee and .14 m from the wrist. These estimates can change only within the one candidate's constraint solve; they are not acceptance claims. Exposed structural members and bearings preserve link length and detail. Use separated overlap-free lamellae around the mechanism, not armor hidden inside another shell. Whole lower-frame, hydraulic, wrist, palm and guard geometry participates in the clearance solve.

**Real support surfaces.** Retain every claw, digit, guard, palm and dewclaw as complete geometry. Add a named flat, detailed rubber sole under each existing paw at the measured claw-tip support plane, with a finite contact area rather than only a tip. Add shallow named ventral bearing skids rigidly owned by spine/pelvis and knee pads owned by the lower links. Initial ventral pad depth is at most .10 source m under the current underside; support geometry must fit the retained body envelope and silhouette. Fold poles are chosen so knees move outward/down into a kneeling support configuration, rather than the repair's upward knees. Solve belly/knee/sole contact together against the full rotated geometry, while all bearings, armor, head and tail remain floor-safe. If these patches cannot bear within the existing proportions, report that exact blocker; do not lift the whole model or claim shadows establish support.

**Connected skeleton and tail.** Preserve the complete 25-joint API, hierarchy, bind centers and limb lengths. The joints are root; pelvis/spine/chest/head; weapon_L/weapon_R/launcher; FL/FR/HL/HR upper/lower/paw; tail_0…tail_4. Rigid armor has exactly one owner. Core heave is one pelvis/carrier transform with child rotations and fixed local connector offsets; independently translating chest/head/waist segments cannot create a load path. All five tail segments follow parent endpoints with fixed lengths and bounded rotation. Independent world placement of each tail segment is retired. The source root remains fixed during the source gate; later native motion alone owns world translation.

## Geometry, articulation and support contract

The preserved rest envelope is x [-1.10,1.10], y [-2.22,3.683], z [.034474,2.495] source m, native scale .8. Overall extrema and recognizable proportions are retained, apart from explicitly named underside bearing geometry within this envelope. Native original radii/heights and Human 3.50 m / actual Scout 3.20 m root separations are unchanged. New posed art outside a native hull still needs world clearance; the long tail is not excused by the original conservative body cylinder.

The allowed joint domain is a connected, constraint-authored path family: quad stance → precontact anticipation → rear load/impact → supported whole-body yield → restraint/claw → withdrawal → supported recovery. It is **not** an arbitrary Euler-angle box. Record each bone's relative angle limits, full armor volume, attachment ownership and interval bounds. Full-body quad folding is the first geometry gate. All owned contact patches must be reachable with fixed lengths, with every other participant surface exterior. A touch may have numerical boundary tolerance; no penetrated exterior is waived. Sampled clearance, closed component containment and swept intervals must all be checked.

Support means actual finite belly/knee/sole patches on the same plane, floor penetration no greater than numerical tolerance (target 1e-4 m), and patch gaps within 1 mm. At least the victim's ventral and/or knee patches must bear, with its pelvis/chest genuinely lowered; paws remain stable. The attacker has planted rear soles and an explicit foreclaw reaction path. Save patch polygons, normals, contact identities, support-slip bounds and a conservative center-of-mass/reaction projection into the support polygon. Any assumed material masses must be stated. Source visual contact alone cannot certify native forces, terrain or roof support.
