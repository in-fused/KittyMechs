# Performance077 — one proposed larger rendering opportunity

Actual user IAB lag on Lowest with Pounce both on and off remains unresolved. CUA still fails before obtaining that app surface. Profile069 is headless Edge main-thread sampling and cannot establish the user's GPU cost, renderer implementation or experience. It must not dismiss that report.

ONE proposed candidate: restore conservative **current animated-pose frustum bounds for enemy skinned meshes**, without throttling animation, contact or native simulation. `Actors.tsx` explicitly sets every cloned SkinnedMesh `frustumCulled=false`. Local Three WebGLRenderer bypasses `Frustum.intersectsObject` under that flag, so an alive enemy model outside the camera still enters draw submission. This is shared by both local Pounce selections: Actors mounts exactly one local rig while all Sentinel enemies retain the legacy Actor path. There is no simultaneous duplicate local actor owner.

The actual069 roster contains twelve alive enemies throughout saved observations: five Scouts, two Mortars and five Anchors. Header-only reads of their retained shipping GLBs show six skinned primitives per actor, totalling72 potential model submissions and188,252 triangles per scene frame. Observed completed renderer counts ranged113–195 calls and795,290–1,159,719 triangles. Thus enemy models represent a substantial source draw inventory; the fraction outside the camera is **unknown**, because069 retained no mesh-level attribution or complete camera pose. All72 cannot be claimed avoidable. The ordinary-view render cost was5307.259 ms inclusive; its renderBufferDirect subtree was3467.099 ms across1150 recorded rAF intervals (~3.015 ms/interval). This overlaps parent render cost and is not actor-only cost or GPU timing. No percentage improvement is measured or promised.

The rejected empty-batch change is excluded. The larger legacy actor callback sampled2836.789 ms inclusive (~2.467 ms/interval), including820.557 ms union of world-position/quaternion getter call stacks (~.714 ms/interval). That getter number avoids nested-parent double counting but includes necessary reads and updates. It does not support changing contact timing or claiming the whole actor cost removable. This diagnosis selects draw admission instead.

Do **not** simply turn on frustum culling with a rest-pose bound, physical hull or per-frame `SkinnedMesh.computeBoundingSphere`: the latter walks every vertex and could increase CPU lag. Proposed bounded future scope is `Actors.tsx` plus one small animated-bounds helper. Prepare bone influence bounds from ALL loaded primitive vertices, ALL four actual weights/indices and ALL skins once. For every positive contributor, store `boneInverse * bindMatrix * vertex` in that bone's influence AABB. On each completed animated/IK/tracking frame, transform its eight corners by current bone world matrices and form a conservative model bound; transform that world bound into each mesh's local sphere and assign that mesh's own `boundingSphere`. Enable ordinary Three frustum culling only for admitted bot meshes. Use pooled math; no geometry mutation, frame React state, per-frame vertex walk or frustum-based tick admission. Renderer tests each main/shadow camera independently, preserving off-camera shadow contributors when shadows are enabled. Ring/telegraph/label ownership stays separate and unchanged.

Containment basis: with nonnegative weights summing to one, the skinned world vertex is a convex combination of its transformed bone-local contributors, so the union world AABB contains it. Actual Float32 weights must be read and validated rather than assumed normalized. Positive finite sums differing from1 require conservative sum-range/bind-affine handling plus shader arithmetic padding; they must not be silently normalized. Missing/negative/nonfinite weights, unsupported morphs, stale ownership/binds, or an unproven case retain the current uncullable visible mesh. Failure must never hide an enemy.

The existing `weightedVisualEnvelope007.ts` source is more than an anchor/witness sampler: its preparation loop visits every loaded position and all weight/index slots for every Mesh, constructs per-positive-contributor bone boxes, retains each skin's actual weight-sum range, and samples those boxes with bind/ownership guards and Float32 arithmetic padding. Its final eight corners describe an aggregate containing those prepared mesh bounds. This gives a relevant implementation/proof starting point, but it has **not** been qualified on all Sentinel assets/actions for this culling use. Reusing camera anchors or eight arbitrary sampled vertices would be insufficient. Require an actual ALL-vertex comparison to the native Three shader-equivalent skin calculation over ordinary enemy travel and turn, paired, climb and death poses, including supports/carrier rotations and actual skin bindings. Keep every applicable limb/contact/action update unchanged. Bounds must follow final tracked head/tail rotations and completed world matrices before render submission; do not use the earlier pre-IK matrix cache.

Added CPU cost is nonzero: setup is O(vertices ×4); steady state is O(used bone/mesh influences ×8), plus bounded matrix checks/local-sphere conversion. All twelve animated actors still pay their current pose/contact work. A whole-model aggregate may be loose but safe; tightness is secondary to preserving every visible vertex. Current evidence cannot quantify this added cost or net savings. If bounds overhead offsets draw savings, stop rather than broaden into animation/contact throttling.
