Pure-translation castShape alone does not prove rotational clearance. Subdivide the yaw arc with a finite bound and inflate each swept sphere by the analytic chord sagitta for that subspan, so the cast covers the arc between tested orientations. Preserve native filter/world/generation/life/owner semantics; query callbacks use captured JS membership only. 3. A stance change checks the full measured transition envelope before acceptance, rather than growing an oriented body into a wall after the core height check. Ground support/step/slope/rubble impulses remain the existing core's job. Clearance lobes are non-supporting queries, but must still reject genuine body-ground/roof penetration; never blanket-ignore upward-facing contacts. If those measurements/contracts are unavailable, that candidate is incomplete and must not ship as an ordinary-only fix.

**Traversal risk:** a physically accurate long body can fit lengthwise through a narrow corridor yet be unable to turn there. That is expected geometry, but arbitrary oversized spheres or an all-action union can unnecessarily block doors, stairs, mantle and paired scenes. Derive stance/action-appropriate bounds, show real minimum corridor/roof clearances, and reject the candidate if ordinary traversability or total locomotion cost worsens without a justified anatomical constraint. A single enlarged radius would worsen every direction and still obscure the underlying pose error; scaling the art down would change the intended character.
