## Why the finite design is justified

The selected original West10 placements, unchanged meter-scale source geometry, yaw0 and ground pivot make a falsifiable integration rather than an unlimited population pass. The useful source silhouettes/detail were accepted narrowly in the prior source review; their clean finish and source-board limitations persist. Ten props cannot repair city masses, horizon or Core presentation.

The declared convex-envelope approximation is legitimate prototype collision when represented honestly. It retains full exported geometry and fills gaps/concavity explicitly, so a fence opening or trolley shelf may be physically blocked. It is neither exact visible contact nor a proof of prop climbing. The design requires native convex hulls for intact host/guest and wreck host/guest and stops on allocation failure, avoiding a silent AABB collider substitution. Conservative AABBs are confined to navigation/broad phase. Physical envelopes may reduce usable passage; ordinary play must judge that actual consequence later.

Finite hollow-equipment game masses are preferable to pretending each convex volume is solid steel. They are declared estimates, with native inertia derived from those masses and existing material/damage/energy policy. A whole-prop damage cell and persistent whole-prop wreck provide an understandable lifecycle without claiming building support, new material fracture or disappearing debris. Retirement of the complete works-pipes and works-crawler banks across rendering, collisions, navigation, support and damage is explicit. Source-placement identities, one-cell banks and a revised world layout can make that supersession deterministic.

The four shared source materials and original node-transform baking preserve visible detail while making batching plausible. Collider-center offsets and one application of wreck quaternion are explicitly tied to the same source-ground frame. Attribution and destination-hash checks are part of the proposed project admission, honestly separate from unavailable CLI package verification.
