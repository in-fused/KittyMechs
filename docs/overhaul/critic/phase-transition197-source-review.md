The remaining visual deficiencies are substantial:

- Gallop has leg extension and recovery, but its armored thorax stays largely straight across the sequence. The deep hock recovery in sample4 and nearly vertical limbs in sample6 are visible; an equally clear axial gather, propulsion release and fore-loading absorption are not. It reads as limb cycling under a relatively rigid body rather than a compelling feline bound. Eight snapshots cannot establish the exact intervening suspension or landing timing.
- Walk/Trot have discernible alternate steps, but the front silhouette and upper body change little. Paw positions near the source floor do not establish purposeful wrist/claw roll or convincing weight acceptance.
- Both upright gaits keep the forearms almost horizontally crossed across the waist/sternum. Full-size UprightStride-front-03 makes this particularly clear. This replaces the old face-level gesture with a lower rigid guard; it still does not read as independent forepaw balance or deliberate opposing shoulder/wrist motion.
- R trunk rotation is clearer and fore return is improved, but the apparent hind support and these arm gestures do not yet convey convincing whole-body load transfer. Source-only flat-floor images cannot establish world tangential contact, native balance or lack of skating.
