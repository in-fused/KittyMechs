## One decision

Replace cell-HP-then-indestructible-wreck ownership with **one persistent bonded material graph shared by attached material and debris**, initially for this brick facade only. Author a finite, conforming solid partition and its mortar/ligament interfaces from the retained source volume and material construction. Give every solid and interface stable IDs. This is a declared material representation, not repeated grid-size or impulse tuning. Unsupported disconnected components become native bodies; still-connected solids remain a compound component. A detached component retains its surviving bonds and can fail further when struck.

Resolve each accepted visible hit against the actual current surface. Accumulate local interface damage from that hit's declared footprint; sever only exhausted interfaces. Split the weapon's existing finite budget between irreversible fracture work and remaining kinetic work, once across every affected owner. A separation must inherit parent pose, linear/angular momentum and exact child mass/centre/inertia; do not grant each child a fresh launch budget. Gravity acts when the graph loses a real load path. Neighbor material stays attached until its actual support or bonds fail. Preserve pre-impact explosion occlusion.

Every parent replacement must be atomic and volume conserving: disjoint children exactly cover the old solid, including thickness; no dust-volume deletion, collider omission, hidden original blocker, artificial opening, or position reset. Render geometry, solid-ray/full-body collision, grip faces and material graph must use those same current owners. Topology revisions retire affected grip leases. Persist graph damage, component poses and IDs to guests and late joiners so a shattered wreck never becomes an intact cell again.

Set a finite authored graph/leaf/collider bound and retain the admitted active-body budget until measured evidence justifies a change. Use compound bodies only for genuinely bonded components. Do not weld loose rubble, freeze unsupported material, relocate piles, or silently discard components to fit capacity. If a valid worst-case fracture cannot fit the declared native budget, this design fails its gate. The current evidence cannot prove that material-conserving debris will leave a full-hull passage: that physical outcome remains the decisive unknown.

## Exact finite implementation gate

Authorize one small brick-facade implementation, not a city-wide fracture rewrite. Freeze its source partition, bond rules, weapon budget, shot/aim sequence, intended passage and native limits before running it; allow one repair of an identified representation defect, then stop if the same route still fails. No fragment-size/impulse sweep.

1. **Source/material:** prove closed solids, exact source-volume/material coverage and mass/inertia accounting; complete initial load paths; finite graph bounds; local bond damage survives detachment and a later hit can split an already-detached owner. An off-target control must retain its undamaged material. No volume disappears at any transition.
2. **Native consequence:** on the exact failed facade, trace the fixed ordinary weapon sequence through first break and continued local failure. Retain every significant debris collider. Demonstrate the unchanged full actor hull crossing the declared passage in both directions after actual debris displacement, plus a deliberately retained obstruction control that still blocks it. Record before/after current-pose surfaces, component IDs, energy/momentum receipts, support loss, sleeping/wake and grip invalidation. A damage counter or camera glimpse of a hole is insufficient.
3. **Persistence/budget:** test another hit on the settled wreck, support removal, guest agreement and late-join reconstruction without intact-cell resurrection or stale grip. Stress the declared maximum concurrent failure and report native step time, active bodies, colliders, snapshots and client frame time against the existing performance contract; a larger finite catalog alone is not a performance qualification.
4. **Ordinary presentation:** both clients must see the impact location progressively weaken, a legible opening form, and retained debris become physical cover/support. Show the actual normal-camera crossing, a blocked control, and debris re-hit without diagnostic teleports or collision exceptions. The independent critic may deny this result despite the source/native checks passing.

Steel/concrete material rules, arbitrary city-wide cascades, character-driven rubble movement, universal breach guarantees and full destruction/art quality remain unqualified. This note does not reopen the exhausted 2x2 candidate.
