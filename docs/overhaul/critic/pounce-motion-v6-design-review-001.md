## Useful evidence and the decisive limitation

The unchanged 117 closed parts now admit 58 newly coupled **samples** with zero moving surface pairs/containment: 33 walk/brake, 17 hind-plant rise and eight turn states. Recorded mesh sole drift is at most .299510 micrometres and seat-axis error .0000105606 degrees. Correct physical FK ownership is a meaningful improvement over the failed independent old knee/paw rotations. The one convergence correction preserved stricter normal/contact requirements and the earlier failed evidence.

However, the 0.16 m stride, .07 m swing lift and .2 m root advance over the four-second walk fixture do not demonstrate an adequate playable gait. A 60-degree quarter rise does not establish an upright complete animal or reverse transition. Sample coordinate envelopes are not independently combinable joint limits. The three traces are disconnected, velocities/rates are zero placeholders, and continuous exterior clearance/recovery is unknown.

**Reject the all-planted small-turn fixture as the next complete motion recipe.** Just four degrees of yaw demands up to .367325 m lateral root displacement and -.217793 m root Y; its .98 reach ratios are explicitly unknown. Two right-side plants can be retained while left paws, belly, head/tail, COM and loads become impossible. This is a constraint warning, not evidence that left limbs can simply be mirrored or that lowering the full root is allowable. Do not widen bounds, copy the quarter root path or collect more samples to turn that fixture into approval.
