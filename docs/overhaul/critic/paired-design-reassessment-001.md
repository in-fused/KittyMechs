# Paired floor-combat design reassessment 001 — October 9, 2026

**Choose one new route: an oblique flank tackle into a side-supported victim, followed by an exterior shoulder restraint and exposed-flank claw beat.** Retire the face-to-face deeply folded kneeling concept. Do not cut another receiving cavity into stopped A/v4 repair-001. This is a design recommendation, not implementation authorization, source qualification or a milestone approval. Full paired floor combat remains incomplete; formal gate counts do not change.

Read-only scope: v4 repair-001 `CONTACT-CONTRACT.md`, `docs/overhaul/pounce-articulation-reassessment.md`, and `shared/pairedCombat.ts`. No new source inspection, render, export, audit, simulation or research was run. Geometry statements below come from those recorded packets.

## Why this route

A failed on upper armor inside shoulder/thigh solids. Socketed v4 plus its sole repair improved those receivers, whole-paw surface clearance and finite floor-bearing geometry, but still placed victim thigh-cowl vertex 2326 **inside its rear palm by 60.698 mm**, and attacker pelvis vertex 16570 inside tail scale 0 by **10.775 mm**. Support reactions, structural walls and continuous clearance remained unknown. These are exterior failures, not bearing-name exemptions.

The repeated concept asks the victim to lower an entire horizontal body through a tight symmetrical fold, packing complete hind paws into hip geometry. A lateral supported roll lowers the body through its native orientation and ordinary limb flex instead. The attacker stays outside the victim's torso, so the design does not depend on placing two body volumes atop each other. The exposed side, different body angles and visible retaining/striking paws give the camera a clearer action. Feasibility is still unproved.

## One concrete choreography and rig

Start with **HumanQuad only**, intact planar ground, one chosen flank. Author one initial approach with attacker heading approximately **60 degrees relative to victim forward** (30 degrees off the flank normal). That angle is a construction target, not an admitted runtime range. Solve the relative root transforms from full-body clearance, reach and support; do not impose 3.50 m spacing, opposite yaws or a closer-root workaround.

1. The attacker approaches the near scapular/flank region through an ordinary moving strike with visible shoulder/foreleg anticipation. Admission requires an actual swept contact there, never mere proximity or a snapped facing pose.
2. The contact transfers linear/angular impulse and tips the victim **away from the attacker** toward an initial roughly 60-degree side roll. Chest and pelvis descend as one connected core. Limbs flex within a modest measured corridor; hind paws remain outside the thigh cowls. Actual floor contacts brake and settle the roll. A collision that cannot reach the supported corridor remains a tackle/stagger with recovery, not an automatically granted pin.
3. The victim bears on finite far-side flank/ventral structural patches plus reachable knee/paw patches. Add only named, mechanically connected pads where the retained silhouette permits them; a decorative pad or four coplanar corners cannot supply a reaction path. Keep the head, optics and tail exterior to floor/partner. The attacker braces rear soles and retains the near shoulder with one complete forepaw; its other forepaw retracts, strikes the exposed flank and returns. Do not use the victim's neck/head as the restraint patch.
4. Escape unloads the retaining paw, clears both complete paw envelopes and lets the victim roll toward a supported stance. Completed release withdraws contact before rise. Both actors retain actual momentum; restore neutral only after real separation permits it.

Build the candidate from preserved masters, retaining feline proportions, material/detail families, skull, canopy, digits, hydraulics and tail. Reuse demonstrated socket principles only as unqualified construction ideas. Use an actual connected core, empty articulation spaces, rigid part ownership and connected tail endpoints. Permit moving armor covers or additional joints when their mechanism/load path justifies them; the earlier 25-joint API is not a user requirement. Supply an explicit migration map for retained action consumers. Do not independently translate chest/pelvis/head or tail segments to fit the pose. One pair pose owner controls the entire articulated presentation.

## Exact authority/presentation contract

The current pair API stores one `anchor`, one `yaw` and a scalar `offset`; `createPair` requires the 3.50/3.20 m spacing bands, and clips contain no root travel. That contract cannot represent this roll and changing oblique relative pose. Replace it deliberately for a **new, versioned quadruped side-pin capability**; existing v3 shoulder-restraint capability remains separately bounded until migration qualifies. Upright, Scout and other cells receive no inherited clearance.

- **Authority owns motion and contacts.** Native root position, full orientation, linear/angular velocity, floor/support contacts and participant constraints are authoritative. Source tracks define joint poses, named contact windows and permitted relative-transform/velocity corridors; they never translate runtime roots. A reduced articulated solver may be used, but it must account for stated masses/inertias and contact/constraint impulses. A scripted post-impact root trajectory that ignores collisions is insufficient. No unconditional partner-collision exclusion or invisible floor carrier.
- **Use conservative pose-aware clearance.** An always-upright body cylinder cannot also describe a side-lying support body. Retain conservative broadphase coverage and introduce oriented, pose-aware full-body narrowphase/support envelopes covering armor, paws, head and tail. Preserve actual native body clearance through each interval. Replacing old circles with smaller radii or simply allowing root overlap does not qualify this route. If the native solver cannot represent the lying body safely, stop before integration.
- **One shared, serializable constraint state.** Versioned choreography/asset IDs, admitted relative transforms and velocities, phase/progress clocks, named partner contact IDs, retained-pressure state, support surface IDs/revisions and contact/beat epochs must accompany reciprocal actor/life ownership. Actor snapshots carry actual native transforms/velocities. Both roles resolve the same source pose and derivative from that state; the renderer applies no locomotion/body-offset layer to an owned pair pose. Strict parsing bounds and reciprocal validation extend to every new field. Late join reconstructs the present state rather than replaying entry.
- **Physical contact owns damage.** Preserve `createPair`'s post-swept-contact boundary and authoritative team/friendly-fire, shield and life eligibility. Existing .28/.8/.35/.13/.24 timings are historical values, not a requirement. Use manifest clocks only where source and native contact actually agree. Combo input requests a beat; apply damage once per accepted contact epoch only when the striking paw meets its named surface and retaining pressure remains valid. Empty strikes or lost pressure cannot damage because an animation clock elapsed.
- **Escape/cancel are joint outcomes.** Retain reciprocal escape/life checks, cancel queued beats immediately on escape, disruption, death/down or invalid support, and release both roles atomically. Arbitrary-time interruption starts from the actual current pose derivative and native velocity, following a bounded withdrawal/braking corridor. No endpoint pop, teleport, synthetic zero velocity or three-fraction cancel proof. Destroyed/moving support revisions must invalidate or update real constraints, never a stale world anchor. If safe recovery is not in the admitted corridor, do not admit the pair.

Source pose progress must remain synchronized with authority constraint progress, within certified tolerances; it cannot run ahead until paws visually penetrate, or lag until damage precedes contact. A failure takes a documented native recovery path and cancels the pair, rather than blending the meshes through the discrepancy.

## Small finite gates, in order

**One candidate plus at most one exact validation repair; then stop.** New work is a new side-pin design, not a continuation of exhausted v4. Do not generate other chassis/cells or a large clip batch first.

1. **Source necessary gate:** show only precontact, first contact, settled side support, retaining/claw contact and fully clear withdrawal poses on the actual two Pounce meshes. Audit all rigid parts bidirectionally for surface crossings and closed containment; floor, articulation topology/walls and complete paw/tail clearance participate. Record support polygons, normals, connected load paths, declared mass/COM assumptions and reaction feasibility. Reject a held failure before expanding motion. Preserve masters/failed packets and comparable source detail.
2. **Continuous source/native gate:** certify the connecting path and arbitrary-clock braking domain with conservative interval bounds, not sparse samples alone. Run one native oblique approach/contact/roll/restraint/withdrawal on intact ground using actual incoming velocities and no state injection. Verify full-body swept clearance, contact impulse, stable floor support, pose-clock agreement and no root snap. Include one miss and one interrupted/escaped branch with pending damage canceled. Only this certified transform/velocity corridor may be advertised; widening it requires evidence.
3. **Normal-camera working acceptance:** uncut ordinary approach and continuing play must expose impact, low supported victim, retaining paw, free claw, distinct escape and recovery. Include a useful oblique normal view as well as the ordinary rear view. The critic can deny passing geometry/native tests if the action still reads as hovering, skating, a symmetric shoulder clamp or a disguised animation carrier. Then verify simultaneous host/guest contact/outcome and late join; no global/milestone approval follows from this small cell.

## Unknowns and hard boundaries

Exact root distances, admissible angles/speeds, side-bearing wall strength, dynamic balance, clearance throughout roll/claw/cancel, deterministic solver cost and camera readability are **UNKNOWN**. The proposed angle/roll do not prove reach or feasibility. The historical 3.50 m opposed-root and exact-25-joint restrictions can be revised explicitly; source/native hashes and migration must record that change. No impossibility claim about all original-hull designs is justified by the failed packets.

If a side-supported body cannot fit a conservative native envelope, if forepaw reach requires exterior penetration, or if support/withdrawal cannot carry actual momentum, stop with exact part/contact/clock witnesses after the finite budget. Do not resurrect deep kneeling cuts, hide geometry, shrink the opponent or weaken friendly-fire/escape/life/network rules. Full tackle/pin, other cells, uneven/roof/damaged support and release acceptance remain required future scope.
