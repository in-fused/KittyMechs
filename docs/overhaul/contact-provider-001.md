## Frozen scope and entry points

`tools/overhaul-contact-provider-001/provider.ts` implements genuine native queries over the supplied actual `RAPIER.World` and its actual collider objects. `fixture.ts` is one finite synthetic actual-native fixture; `tsconfig.json` scopes its independent type check. It neither constructs an alternate runtime world nor owns physics stepping in production. The fixture alone owns its test world.

`NativeContactProvider(world, generation)` binds one unique world object and never-reused generation for this process. `register(id, revision, collider)` requires a live collider from that world; owner IDs are stable and revisions increase strictly after retirement. Collider identity, parent identity, shape, enabled/sensor state, and current world membership are checked. Native handles alone do not identify an owner. `retire` invalidates the current query frame. Geometry/attachment changes without a new owner revision are rejected. `dispose` invalidates the provider; world freeing remains the actual world owner's responsibility.

`beginFrame(clock, motions)` receives trusted scene-authority facts following one committed physics step: generation, integer monotonic tick, native simulation seconds, dt, and every participating owner's committed linear displacement over the next bounded interval. The first clock is an attestation; Rapier exposes no public authoritative step counter here. Subsequent clocks must be consecutive, with matching dt and `world.timestep`, at most 1/30 second. Lifecycle changes do not reset clock history. Provider-minted frame objects are opaque capabilities; stale or foreign frame objects fail. All enabled non-sensor world colliders must have active registered identities and committed motion. Disabled bodies/colliders and sensors participate in neither support nor clearance. Scene obstacle angular motion is unsupported and rejected. A signature snapshot before/after each admitted query rejects mutation within a frame. Direct per-collider native ray and shape queries avoid stale scene broadphase queries and native reads within native callbacks.

The caller's committed motion must be the complete actual interval path (linear translation, fixed scene-collider orientation). A motor command or a future guess alone does not establish this condition. No claim is made about uncommitted future input, acceleration, deviation from that trajectory, or between-step scene geometry replacement. This is conditional finite geometry evidence, not a force or motion-control certificate.

## Units and finite support proof

World points/displacements require the runtime tag `native-m-y-up`; authored input requires `authored-unit-z-up`. The explicit canonical right-handed boundary is **native = (.8 sourceX, .8 sourceZ, -.8 sourceY)**, with an inverse. This conversion does not apply the actor mount or carrier. The existing presentation mount is a separate adapter responsibility. Collider-local and pad-local positions, unit normals, and measured metres/second velocity have separate output tags. No untagged mixed-space vector enters the public query API.

`samplePad(frame, pad)` measures a horizontal yaw-oriented actual cuboid pad volume, specified by native volume center, positive half extents, and yaw. An authored sole must become the native volume center by adding native +Y half-height exactly once. The profile is frozen: 25mm upward ray start, 50mm downward range, 2mm contact gap, 0.1mm maximum penetration, 1mm minimum face-edge margin, and 10µm plane consistency tolerance. These are geometry query tolerances, not tuned gait or load values.

Five actual native nearest-hit rays retain their raw native points, normals, distances and owner keys. Admission additionally requires the same actual cuboid face at every corner and center, an upward horizontal normal, agreement with the finite actual collider-local face, and strict containment of all four corners in that face. A convex planar cuboid-face rectangle containing all four convex sole corners contains the whole sole, including its interior. Thus the finite region proof is not inferred from isolated rays or an analytic scene floor. Unsupported mesh/heightfield/rounded/nonhorizontal/compound support is rejected, even when all sample rays happen to hit it. A native `Collider.contactShape` result admits only the stated gap/penetration envelope, and native shape intersection rejects another collider penetrating the pad volume.

The admitted witness preserves actual current collider pose, collider-local anchor and normal, native world anchor and normal, all five native ray receipts, the native contact receipt, finite edge margin, owner/generation/revision and one exact clock. It proves geometric pad proximity/coverage within this explicit envelope. It does **not** prove a plant carries weight, friction, actuator force, support impulse, COM containment or hind bearing.

`transport(currentFrame, previousWitness)` accepts only this provider's immediately previous admitted support. It checks current owner lifecycle and transports the recorded collider-local anchor through the actual current collider transform. It reports actual measured average world velocity over the last committed dt, not an instantaneous velocity or acceleration. Transport does not renew a support contact; call `samplePad` at the new position before claiming current contact. A retired, disabled, foreign, reused, changed-shape or stale owner fails.

## Finite swept envelope proof

`sweep(frame, pad, motion, clearance)` preserves one actual native `Shape.castShape` receipt for every registered participating scene collider. No support collider is excluded: a pad's loaded floor contact can block clearance. Scene collider shapes are conservatively limited to cuboids; unsupported geometry rejects the complete query. Each native cast covers normalized time [0,1], exactly the declared dt, using both the pad and scene collider committed translations. Initial overlap and target clearance are included (`stopAtPenetration=true`). A rejection preserves the complete candidate's native hits and local witnesses, normals, fractions, owner identity, actual current pose and committed motion.

For fixed yaw, the exact pad cuboid is cast. For any nonzero declared pad yaw path bound (0..2π), an axis-aligned square prism with horizontal half extents `hypot(padHalfX,padHalfZ)` and the actual pad half height encloses the pad at **every** yaw throughout the interval. Its native translational cast therefore conservatively covers yaw while retaining genuine native collision witnesses. A prism hit can be a false positive for the smaller actual pad; it is still a rejection, never a permission to ignore the hit. Pad tilt, scene rotation, arbitrary articulated motion and curved translational paths are unsupported. The envelope is one pad volume, never complete skin/armor/head/body coverage.

Architecture mapping is explicit: current pad and geometric local transport can be wrapped into the transition owner's contract, with a trusted injective mapping if its generation is numeric. Find-plant policy is not implemented. Physical `release`, `loadSupport`, whole `bodySweep`, instantaneous acceleration and native controller R remain unsupported. Existing KCC grounding, prescribed floors and animation flags cannot fill those unknowns. Climbing, paired combat, retained actions, native body/controller integration, two-client ordinary play and camera approval remain pending.
