# View aim and pair camera

`InputState.aimYaw?: number` separates accepted crosshair heading from movement/orbit `yaw`. `parseInput` rejects supplied nonfinite/non-numeric aim atomically; omission normalizes to finite legacy `yaw`. `emptyInput` deliberately omits aim, allowing existing mutable bot inputs to continue following their own yaw. Native actor aim and immutable strike receipt use `input.aimYaw ?? input.yaw`. Existing movement direction, body movement yaw, pair lifecycle, strike proof and projectile collision remain unchanged.

`CameraRig` resolves the actual cleared camera's central ray against native cover and the existing presented actor hit cylinders. `crosshairMuzzleAim` uses the presented local root and the exact native shoot offset: horizontal `direction * 1.3`, vertical `.65` quadruped or `1.65` upright. A bounded 28-step monotonic solve aligns pitch with that offset; yaw follows the actual world target. Targets inside the muzzle offset or beyond existing pitch limits are reported unreachable, rather than claimed as exact hits. `controls.shotYaw`/`shotPitch` enter `readInput`; owner/lifecycle resets retire the sampled shot yaw.

`cameraPairOther` admits only reciprocal live life-bound pairs at actual root separation <= 8 m and height difference <= 2 m. No proximity pairing, actor movement or contact waiver occurs. Pair focus is halfway between presented roots, bounded to 2.5 m from the local focus. A side-view bias is additive to unrestricted player yaw and capped at 0.7 radians. Desired focus/yaw ease at 5 Hz with extra hard caps of 3 m/s and 1.2 rad/s. Actual currently rendered envelopes of both participants supply the union fit; absent/stale envelope data is reported unavailable. Boom extension is capped at 4 m and 3 m/s. Pair acquisition and release do not add a snap. The pre-existing reset/new-local-visual snap is unchanged.

Deliberate orbit speed over .25 rad/s suppresses framing for .3 s after the last look change. Aiming and steep pitch fade the bias. Focus and final interpolated camera both retain the existing exact native sphere sweep, even if cover prevents a useful pair frame. The caps describe the additional framing motion, not unrestricted user orbit, actor travel, camera shake or obstruction correction. Actual intermediate motion and contact readability still require root's frozen browser capture.

With `?debug=1`, the existing read-only camera diagnostic gains top-level `movementYaw`, `lookYaw`, `lookPitch`; movement/navigation harnesses should use `movementYaw`, never `Actor.aimYaw`. `pairFraming` contains `id`, `active`, `yawBias`, copied `focusOffset`, `distanceExtra`, `bothVisuals`, `fitted`, `lookHold`, `aimYaw`, `aimPitch`, `aimReachable`. Pair fit is a desired unconstrained fit; existing `clear`/`violations` describe native camera clearance. Capture consecutive camera positions/focus and these fields to measure motion rather than infer it from constants.

Focused qualification files: `tests/overhaul-view-aim.test.ts` (schema, exact muzzle witness, twin native movement parity, projectile and admitted strike heading); `tests/overhaul-view-input.test.ts` (installed controls and keyboard sampling); `tests/overhaul-pair-camera.test.ts` (reciprocity, acquisition/release limits, deliberate look freedom, projected center separation and native obstruction). No browser visual or performance approval is claimed here.

The bounded correction preserves production behavior and asserts the rate/response-derived release tail and exact authored cuboid/sphere separation instead. The final rerun remains root-owned.
