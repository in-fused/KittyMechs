## Native/render integration gate

- Preserve the existing fixed-step strike sweep and qualified source clocks. Replace immediate impact velocity zeroing only for a qualified A entry with finite dissipating shared translation derived from accepted same-tick incoming travel. Sweep both original full hulls through the real world and prove pair-relative full-shape nonpenetration at every bounded substep. Excluding the partner from external queries is allowed only after that separate exact proof; never use a blanket collision waiver. Commit accepted roots together. Obstruction or a ledge may truncate or decline A; never correct roots to make a clip fit.
- Enter the pin only when real native support and source-derived actual sole/knee/belly/contact footprints agree. Validate support ownership and normals on terrain, native roofs and live structural/ruin faces. Art extending beyond the original hull needs source-derived world-clearance proof too. A canonical flat-floor pose is not permission to pin on arbitrary slopes, missing floor or an unsupported roof. Loss of support invalidates the constraint immediately.
- Use one source contact packet for authority proof and the single render pose owner. Include immutable asset/geometry/bind/clip hashes, role/stance/opponent cells, original hulls/scales, actual support footprints and claw/target triangle ownership, dense phase samples, swept envelope/intersection receipts and recovery transitions. Do not declare global qualification from source-only measurements. Reciprocal life/phase clocks and accepted motion/support ownership must be strictly admitted in snapshots and reproduced by guests.
- Escape/cancellation clears pending claw damage and pressure, retains accepted roots, and recovers continuously from the current pose while native movement remains swept. Death, disconnect, interruption and support retirement invalidate ownership immediately. Damage requires an actual qualified contact beat; an animation or queued timestamp alone cannot establish it. Preserve the observed v3 fallback where qualified; unsupported geometry retains ordinary native strikes.

After the source gate, qualify the native path on real flat ground and an owned roof, plus wall/ledge blockage, support loss, cancellation, escape, interruption and late join. Then obtain ordinary host/guest evidence using the actual source meshes, support, contact, pose clocks and accepted root motion. Joint arithmetic, source images and unit tests cannot approve satisfying tackle/pin presentation by themselves.
