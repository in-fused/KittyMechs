The wrist should narrow, bend and widen into the paw naturally; no constant-height cover, vertical jagged cut, flat toe fronts or independent sole roller rows. Center toes are slightly longer and fuller than outside toes. Preserve the detailed feline face and overall limb rhythm. Matte dark flexible core, fitted pale/dark-metal dorsal/side plates, small copper actuator/link cues and polished compact hooks should read with the already retained materials. ## Intended interfaces and rejection rules

Connected anatomy sharing welded boundaries and contiguous pad/core material is intentional, as are explicitly modeled armor mounts and a real claw hinge. These require shared/mapped topology or named geometric mating surfaces. No blanket same-owner/under-skin intersection exception. Forbidden: unrelated retained armor crossing core, external plates crossing one another/body away from their declared mounts, claw touching recess/core throughout its swing, foot skin/pads/claws below the frozen floor, and joint parts intersecting to fabricate a load path. Distinct material volumes remain explicit; an actual deforming core must not be treated as a rigid filled collider carrying a free-moving claw inside it. Check both surface crossings and actual retained→new-solid containment; reverse multi-solid union unknowns cannot certify clearance.
