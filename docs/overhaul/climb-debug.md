# Gantry climb correction

The original two Pounce failures are native geometry failures. Neither actor attached during 960 accepted climb/forward ticks. The east relay produced two valid front-paw receipts (`gripMask=3`), but its complete attachment cylinder intersected `entry-gantry:column-1-1` at `(15,4.12,-12.3)`. That pylon extends 0.4m outside the relay facade. The gantry west face at `x=-19` owned only elevated beam end caps; its actual pylons have exterior plane `x=-19.4`. Its outward west approach also lies inside the west relay footprint. Increasing timers or admitting unowned contacts would conceal these defects.

The correction uses one visible, bank-owned steel portal panel on the north side of the bridge: center `(5,4.27,-15.8)`, half extents `(2.5,4.35,0.2)`, exterior contact plane `z=-16`, floor `-0.08`, top `8.62`. Its left edge `x=2.5` clears a 2.05m hull walking along the main road center `x=0`; the spawn corridor at `x=-8` is also clear. Existing pylons, beams and deck support remain physical and destructible. The panel depends on existing beam support and retires through the existing graph. It is a facade cell rendered from the same source box, not a carrier proxy.

`shared/overhaulGantryPortal.ts` exports the panel constants, `overhaulPortalRailSpan(minX,maxX)` and `overhaulClimbFaces(building,blocks)`. The latter returns only exact authored portal faces for this sparse gantry, and `null` for ordinary buildings. Each contact owner must have the exact exterior anchor plane. Native five-point paw proof, measured normal, reach limits, full climb hull and five-point roof landing proof remain required by Simulation. The portal's roof is still the six real deck cells. Clip only the north rail segments to leave a real five-meter gap (`x=2.5..7.5`); keep their original bank IDs and shortened cuboids. Full deck collision remains present beneath the gap.

Root integration:

1. In district compilation, create one `portal-bank` cell `portal-frame`, facade/steel/transit, HP160, supported by either existing beam. Assign its anchor ID `entry-gantry:grip-south`, outward normal `(0,0,-1)`, position `(5,-0.08,-16)`, width5, height8.7, approach `(5,-0.08,-19)`, landing `(5,8.92,-14.17)`. Replace invalid end-pylon climb anchors with this portal anchor; preserve pylons as real structural support.
2. Use `overhaulPortalRailSpan` for each north rail's existing segment interval. Preserve south rails and all deck geometry.
3. Before ordinary envelope construction in `buildingFaces`, call `overhaulClimbFaces`; append returned faces and skip the continuous-envelope recipe when non-null.
4. Move only the east relay's default west-anchor tangent to `z=-8` (position, landing, approach). The full facade's immutable envelope and exact collision boxes remain intact.
5. Legacy parameterized fixture placement must offset both X and Z by `anchor.normal*(radius+.3)`. Its gantry walk after a side mantle must follow the physical longitudinal deck (`yaw=-PI/2`); walking 7m straight inward deliberately leaves a 4m deck. Keep the original mantle, grounded and full-floor assertions.

The focused regression `tests/overhaul-climb-debug.test.ts` sends parsed ordinary inputs through the unchanged native authority. It covers both nearby grips and a mission-spawn navigation walk to the portal. It checks actual attachment, unsupported out/lift masks, full-hull transfer height, mantle completion within the existing 960-tick bound, remaining energy above35, native landing and more than3m continued longitudinal bridge walking. Instrumentation records rejected owners without changing query outcomes. Navigation returns snapped grid centers; the route case finishes its last few meters with ordinary steering toward the authored approach before requesting climb. No actor pose, mask, native query or collider is overridden.
