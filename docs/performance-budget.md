The current recon/medic art comparison uses quiet serial one-pilot Rainline dedicated-PVP runs on the same GTX 1070 Ti, medium1440×900 and camera10. Every moving baseline/candidate run retains one >100ms gap (about165–172ms); these short samples establish comparable scoped cost, not a timing gain, hitch-free play or minimum specification.

The matched old roster plateaus at126/59/175 and123/56 after retirement. The extra maps remain cached, while actor skeleton resources retire; repeated cycles show no growth in this bounded scenario. Its camera14/resource captures are separate from the quiet camera10 timing runs and do not qualify crowd FPS, mobile hardware or VRAM.

These short samples show comparable throughput on this PC; they do not establish an FPS improvement, minimum specification, worst-case combat performance or physical-phone qualification. The fresh Pounce/Rainline candidate averages **243.3 draw calls and 1,262,345.6 triangles**, including shadow passes, versus **243.3 calls and 1,262,353.6 triangles** in the same-session frozen environment release. Geometry/texture counts settle at **146/57** in both. Turn clips add about 50–62 KiB per player rig and runtime pose/contact work; mesh/skin/index data and every old clip channel remain exact after decoding. The environment's earlier increases in foliage/architecture cost remain documented in its retained artifact. The far-terrain underlay grounds distant scenery with one coarse batch. High uses a larger foliage radius and substantially more triangles. Counters accumulate across all passes before resetting each frame. Shared skeletons consolidate bone-texture allocation across material primitives.

Current guardrails: models under 5 MB; playable mechs about 15–16.3k triangles and 819–867 KiB compressed with 28 clips; scanned rock around 2.2k; textures generally 512–1024 px; HDR 1k; bounded particle/projectile pools. The detailed factory is 17,143 triangles/3,961,572 bytes. Fir near/mid/far levels cost 23,111/10,044/4,514 triangles; the two fern variants cost 2,099/480 and 816/480. All levels load together, share original matched PBR materials and use instanced batches with four-meter hysteresis. Solid trees always retain a visible level; only near trees cast shadows on supporting presets. Paw contact uses at most twenty static casts per actor on a 30 Hz probe deadline and revalidates cached handles each frame; it changes visual rigid-joint placement without changing authority or extending limb lengths. At most twelve authoritative fields and sixteen transient area bursts render; terrain rims are reused across snapshot updates and disposed at expiry. Weather shares preset particle budgets and adds one procedural sky draw. Service facades/decks/ribs/markers use three instanced batches and ground-conforming approach markings. Cosmetic cover rubble uses 32/64/96 instances by quality and expires after 3.5–5 seconds. Stable destroyed-ID signatures avoid rebuilding meshes or guest physics on 20 Hz HP snapshots. Instancing shares prop geometry across chunks. No per-frame React state update controls transforms. The movement timeline retains eight pooled samples per actor; worker terrain retains at most 96 completed CPU entries, and scene geometry remains explicitly disposed. All collidable trees are shown at every preset, adding real visible foliage cost rather than leaving invisible trunks.

Matched screenshot-free turn and duty-reversal routes measured **15.045 seconds** for the repaired candidate: p50 **17.5 ms**, p95 **18.6 ms**, p99 **19.3 ms**, maximum **25 ms**. Frozen 240baeb measured **15.121 seconds**, p50 **17.5**, p95 **18.4**, p99 **19.0**, maximum **20.2 ms**. Both had zero frames over 50/100 ms, camera violations or errors and settled at **104 geometries/50 textures**. These short routes qualify the exercised turn cost, not terrain streaming, long-session endurance or eight-player combat.

Historical environment evidence remains: its screenshot-free medium moving coast route recorded 2,057 RAF frames over 36.1845 seconds, p50 17.6 ms/p95 18.5/p99 19/max 37.9, zero frames over 50/100 ms. Its 218.0065-second capture-inclusive three-return route plateaued at 104 geometries/50 textures, p99 19.4 ms/max 189/two frames over 100 ms. Those measurements are retained baseline evidence and are not relabeled as fresh authored-turn runs.

Repeated expedition changes are browser-tested against geometry growth. Physics chunk tests verify that returning to the same region restores the original body count. Instanced batch buffers, terrain, cloned materials, mixer actions, weather and bodies/controllers are released. GLB/texture loader caches remain until the page closes, so memory should plateau after the asset set is loaded rather than return to zero.

Remaining costs: leaf overdraw, high-preset foliage triangles and large renderer/physics JavaScript chunks. The Rapier/WASM bundle is about 2.26 MB minified (838 kB gzip), and the Three/R3F bundle about 1.25 MB (354 kB gzip). Next measured optimizations are further foliage alpha/overdraw tuning, material consolidation and KTX2 texture delivery. Verify GPU memory and 95th-percentile frame time before setting a production minimum spec.
