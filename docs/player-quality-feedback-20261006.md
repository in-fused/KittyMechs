# Player quality feedback — October 6, 2026

Read this alongside `resume-after-usage-reset.md` before resuming development. This records new user feedback after the saved playable release; it does not alter sealed release evidence or claim a fix. The continuous goal remains paused.

The user reports that the current public game looks and plays poorly: climbing is clunky, combat is weak and clunky, feline tackling/pouncing/wrestling is not convincing, buildings do not visibly degrade or destruct, and rooftops look glitchy and static. The user has approximately 7% weekly usage remaining and is considering abandoning this implementation or using another model/service. Treat the lost confidence and poor actual play experience as the highest-priority product issue.

## Required change in admission criteria

Technical assertions and independent source review remain useful but do not establish movement feel, combat quality or visual fidelity. The existing critic approval is limited to the recorded release scope. Physical rubble was disabled; realistic collapse and the private denser city candidate were not shipped. Do not imply those requests are complete.

Before another expansion or quality claim, demonstrate one coherent playable street and rooftop encounter with one chassis. Judge ordinary input and a normal camera, using actual gameplay footage and user evaluation. This is a proposed recovery slice, not newly authorized execution while the goal is paused.

- **Traversal:** fluid acceleration/turning and transformation; reliable jumps onto varied facade locations; clear claw purchase; movement up a wall and over the roof edge; stable roof contact and camera; no visible snapping, hovering or stuck transitions. Test building corners and approach angles, not just scripted central routes.
- **Combat:** an aimable pounce that visibly connects; a tackle with readable body contact and displacement; a claw/grapple follow-up; expressive attacker/victim reactions; recovery and escape. Distinguish simulation commands from a convincing feline combat sequence. Include a resisting enemy rather than only a stationary target.
- **Environment:** visible localized damage at claw/landing/attack contacts, pieces detaching where hit, collision changing consistently, and readable destruction progression. Do not label cosmetic particles as physical degradation or full structural collapse. Resolve rooftop glitches in the actual released assets/geometry.
- **Visual quality:** cohesive materials, lighting and silhouettes under the ordinary gameplay camera. Inspect moving footage; menu art and isolated model renders cannot establish in-game quality.

Record player-visible failures and measured constraints plainly. Keep the failed public Co-Op smoke as a separate unresolved regression. Prioritize the highest-value visible fixes over additional infrastructure, broad asset generation, enormous maps or repeated numerical diagnostics. Retain the repository and paid asset bank for reuse. No model change, rewrite, new subscription, spend or automatic resumption has been authorized by this feedback alone.

On explicit resumption, read the prior handoff for recovery packages, process safety and frozen candidate states. Refresh source/service identities and propose a bounded implementation scope with a concrete playable acceptance demonstration. Do not claim AAA completion, guaranteed improvement, or absence of regressions.
