## Change

Close views previously cut off paws, tails and much of the upright body. The camera now uses an envelope prepared from the actual shipping model's weighted vertices and static meshes. Preparation scans geometry once; each frame samples reused bone/mesh corners after the final animation, strike, IK and head/tail pose. The frame order is Physics -2, Session -1.5, city/guest survival presentation -1.25, Actor -1, then camera 0. Exact pose clock, root identity and session generation gates reject stale owners.

The fitter uses the current perspective lens and viewport, a 5% frame reserve, a bounded search of at most23 probes and the existing18m horizontal boom cap. The camera distance setting is a preferred minimum in ordinary views, so a6m request can expand to fit a long feline silhouette. Look lead becomes2m instead of6m; existing4m boom height and16m pitch reach remain. The first camera/model arrival can snap; outward fit demand is immediate at the distance filter while camera position still eases at16Hz. Inward relaxation uses3Hz. Physical cover clearance, recoil, native simulation, authored animations, models, claws, grip profiles and dependency versions are preserved. Production co-op aiming helpers use the new lead and actual displayed horizontal distance.

Attached skin inverses must match Three's actual current inversion without normalization; computed inverse footer rounding is handled separately from strict authored forward matrices. Unit cases cover weighted skins, transformed/nested meshes, shared bones, interleaved geometry, unsupported bindings and owner replacement. New geometry or changed attribute/binding versions require re-preparation.

## Limits and next work

Framing, shot and quiet producers use medium graphics with reduced flashes/camera shake. Default-shake and recoil-frame coverage remains unqualified. These settings are not attributed to the separately executed unchanged regression specs.

`fitAvailable` describes the solver's ideal position, not a guarantee after position smoothing, recoil or physical-cover compression. Steep aiming and camera collision can legitimately hide parts of the mech; clearance and view direction keep priority. The18m cap is a horizontal boom parameter, not Euclidean camera-focus distance. Initial warmed samples named `initial-reset` do not establish an actual reset, death or respawn. Full route continuity, model-arrival/reset/respawn, moving orbit/rapid turns, close-cover aimed target fire, every map/weapon and WAN remain further qualification work. New art, city population, universal climbing and structural collapse are separate requirements; this camera change does not grant them AAA or release approval.
