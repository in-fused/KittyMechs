## Repaired findings

- Inactive enemies could still obstruct the character controller. Disabled corpse colliders are now excluded consistently, including deaths during the same authority tick; living walls retain collision.
- Frostline checkpoint candidates could become perpetually pending because slope support queried the wrong full-hull condition. Terrain support and final static/occupant clearance now admit safe, bounded positions without bypassing buildings or other pilots.
- Low/medium density could hide collidable trees. All physical props now stay visible at every quality; density reduction applies to noncollidable ground foliage.
- Main-thread terrain construction and an early underlay cutout could reveal missing ground during streaming. Worker-built terrain, bounded CPU caching, predictive neighborhoods and ready-tile underlay retirement keep ground available while detailed tiles arrive.
- Snapshot cadence could step movement and desynchronize body/camera. A shared presentation timeline buffers guests, samples local authority at 60 Hz, handles discontinuities, and advances gait from visible distance rather than wall-clock animation alone.
- Cosmetic Rainline water covered reachable terrain and hid the mech; the unsupported mountain shell also overlapped the square movement envelope. Shared scenery placement moves the ocean beyond the western limit and mountains beyond all square corners, with a 32 m clearance for the camera. Boundary regression and independent compiled coast acceptance pass: the same legal position now shows the full mech and textured terrain, with zero camera violations.
- The post-mantle form input expired before the landing/retraction finished. Its short intent window now spans both authored transitions; pause, blur and death still clear input.

## Practical limits

This is an improved playable prototype, not commercial AAA qualification. Four chassis have different silhouettes, cadence, poses and abilities, but arbitrary obstacle paw IK, more natural authored transitions and production architecture/material sets remain work.

Climbing supports two marked workshop facades and complete roof decks per theater. Scouts can pursue onto those roofs; optional guarded uplinks and rooftop cover add combat. Destruction covers admitted walls and cargo with matching replicated collider/model retirement; arbitrary building fracture and general climbing are not implemented.

PeerJS and WebSocket multiplayer are exercised on this PC. Remote household NAT/TURN, poor-network input prediction, host backgrounding, eight-player stress and long sessions are not qualified. The temporary friends route needs this PC, arena and tunnel alive; durable hosting remains deferred. Touch tests use desktop Edge emulation, not physical phone hardware.

Dynamic weather changes visuals, lighting and fog; it does not change traction, AI perception or damage. Short throughput samples do not provide frame-time percentiles, minimum hardware requirements or memory endurance evidence. Cosmetic tails/claws/antennae extend beyond conservative body hulls; projectile physics uses actual colliders while crosshair acquisition uses a finite-cylinder proxy.

The original Unreal folder stays read only. Its deletion condition remains unmet.
