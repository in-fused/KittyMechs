## Three-piñata private experiment

The isolated lab lives in `.sites-runtime/destruction-lab-001`, with exact npm three-piñata 2.0.1 and Three 0.180.0 installed using `--ignore-scripts`. The project's package.json and lockfile are unchanged. Its synthetic closed-panel fixtures do not replace source-building collision or count toward universal destruction.

Source inspection found specific integration obligations:

- Fixed seed and custom local seed points are available. Omitted seeds use Math.random. Same-process repeatability is a bounded check; cross-browser bit equality is unproved. Ship baked buffers and content hashes instead of independently fracturing on every client.
- Full Voronoi neighbor clipping has quadratic seed-pair work. Approximation can overlap pieces. Keep fragment count bounded and move fracture preparation out of the gameplay tick.
- Input must be watertight. Do not close genuine doors/cavities by substituting a whole-building convex hull or box. Admit individual real closed components with source triangle/material provenance.
- Fragment centers use matrixWorld while quaternion/scale are copied locally. Flatten geometry for an identity bake, then apply its admitted owner transform once; test nested transforms explicitly.
- Outer/inner materials are shared references. The library's per-mesh disposal also disposes materials, so ownership must dispose each geometry and shared material separately.
- Local removal, surviving grips, roof support, collider retirement, navigation and replicated masks must agree before visual fragments become gameplay. A support graph and physical rubble are separate required work.

Measure finite positions, triangle degeneracy, geometric edge incidence, volume conservation, material identity, repeated output hashes and bake times. Retain failures instead of changing tolerances to make a candidate pass. Follow with true fragment collision, directional impulse, resting/cleanup behavior and a bounded multiplayer lifecycle test before runtime adoption.

The first single bake ended with exit1 because the custom 2.5D case failed: both repeats matched, but fragments 5 and 6 (two of seven) had degenerate/boundary/non-manifold edge diagnostics and total volume fell short by 0.9221556%. This path is not admitted. The seeded 3D case produced seven pieces/430 triangles, repeated hashes matched, no topology invalids were detected and relative volume error was 2.58e-8. Its two bake times were 9.34/4.13 ms on this machine. These are tiny synthetic fixtures, not a browser frame budget or real-building qualification. An open plane was rejected by the lab's topology preflight; that is not a library-wide rejection guarantee. Full immutable results: `.sites-runtime/destruction-lab-001/attempt-001/receipt.json`; execution/source binding seal: `.sites-runtime/destruction-lab-001/execution-001/seal.json`. No retries or runtime changes were made for the failed fracture path.

The independent critic verified execution/source hashes, raw 3D topology, positive per-piece volumes, material identity flags and the retained 2.5D defects. Approval is limited to conditional offline exploration. Exact source components, overlap/self-intersection, transforms, material/provenance retention, true physical collision, support, replication, lifecycle and rendered quality are not admitted by this result.
