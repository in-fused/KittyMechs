## Release gates and limits

Current gates include cold fallback and Higgsfield fixture handoff, ordinary climbs and destruction, guest/late-join persistence, transport compatibility, matched performance, render teardown, regression checks and all four critic areas approved against the final fingerprint. Review receipts and frozen version11 evidence remain authoritative until the candidate passes.

This is one compact industrial district, one refined player chassis and finite damage cells with one dependent roof sector. It does not yet fulfill a vast populated city, arbitrary voxel fracture, climb/destruction for every world prop, a cinematic transformation or AAA art. Continued work should deepen material/model detail and distinct city silhouettes after these foundations qualify. Durable multiplayer hosting remains deferred.
