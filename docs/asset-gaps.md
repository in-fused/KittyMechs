# Remaining production gaps

- Seven 25-bone rigs carry 28 player/17 Sentinel clips for travel, angular turn steps, forms, traversal, recoil, abilities, hit reactions and collapse. Chassis differ in silhouette, stride/duty and authored combat poses. A shared presentation timeline, distance-driven gait, actual fixed-surface paw anchors and rigid joint correction improve continuity/contact. Production silhouettes/materials, further turn blending, authored acceleration/braking and landing transitions, dynamic-platform support, arbitrary uneven contact and physical ragdolls still need work. Cosmetic tails/antennae/tufts are outside core hull qualification.
- Each theater has two authored climbs, an optional guarded rooftop uplink, supported Scout pursuit, one breakable wall and two roof cargo banks. Arbitrary building scaling, structural fracture, persistent physical rubble and navigation over general stacked interiors remain ahead.
- Co-op relay/core checkpoints support safe respawns and late joins. Six randomized beneficial supply caches add repair, energy and cooldown recovery. Downed/revive, varied cooperative objective mechanics, campaign persistence and salvage spending remain to be built.
- Three maps have distinct graded terrain, roads, objectives, routes and landmarks while sharing expedition rules. Detailed Rainline workshops preserve existing climb/deck proxies; richer map-wide architecture, mission scripting, enemy formations and pacing with friends remain priorities.
- Matched scanned PBR sets, instanced fir/fern LODs, generated albedos and simple retained props coexist. Larger environmental variety, alpha/overdraw tuning, texture compression/atlases and production lighting still need work. No claim of completed AAA art is made.
- Higgsfield concept illustrations/menu footage do not supply gameplay meshes or collision. Three 256 px supply faceplates and a 512×290 workshop sign are integrated runtime textures with retained prompts/jobs/normalization receipts. Actual chassis portraits come from playable model sources. The connector currently lacks a callable 3D generation tool; catalog availability is not a completed mesh job.
- Longer eight-player sessions, physical mobile hardware, poor-network behavior and durable hosting remain unqualified or deferred. Larger animation libraries also increase downloads; source regeneration requires the local asset snapshot.
- No browser runtime reads the old Unreal directory. Preserve provenance and exact licensing with every added source. The original remains read-only because unimplemented design details and rejected art history still have reference value; its deletion condition remains unmet.
