# Art direction

KittyMech pairs a small cat pilot with a mechanically credible feline silhouette. Four planted paws support a low articulated body; shoulders and hocks bend differently, the spine moves through the bound, and the tail balances it. Armor plates, exposed joints, hydraulic detail, cockpit glass and restrained luminous optics carry the identity.

The four editable chassis distinguish silhouette and motion. Pounce is a compact springy skirmisher with deep crouches and a crossed claw rake. Bastion carries broad shoulder/flank shields and heavy weapons, short weighted strides and a planted shield brace. Lynx has a slim teal recon frame, longer limbs, rangefinder mast, ear tufts, quicker diagonal strides and asymmetric emitter poses. Caracal carries pale medic armor, orange accents, tall ears and green repair cells, with steady support steps and a protective repair cradle. Actual-model portraits use these same Blender sources. Stray is a reference for weight shifts, responsive transitions and plausible contact, following [BlueTwelve’s account of its animation work](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=367025); designs and assets remain original. Its finished animation quality is an ambition, not a quality level established by this prototype.

All seven rigs contain 25 bones. Each player has 26 clips and each Sentinel has 15: quadruped travel, wall traversal, neutral combat base, pulse/missile/ability/hit/death reactions, plus player upright travel, transforms and upright combat reactions. Accepted authority timestamps select additive reactions with death, ability, missile, pulse and hit priority. Continuous damage cannot hide firing recoil. Source evaluated core bounds and actual optimized GLB sampling qualify the conservative hulls; tails, whiskers, antennae and tufts remain cosmetic. Collapse is authored rather than a physics ragdoll. A coherent body/camera timeline and distance-synchronized phase avoid 20 Hz stepping and gait drift. Authored duty timing smoothly releases swing paws during slow walks. The models still share feline gait families; precise obstacle-aware paw locking, richer transitions and production polish remain ahead.

Rainline is an industrial coastal dock district, Ember a terraced basalt quarry, and Frostline a rising alpine storm station. Their terrain, road networks, objectives, patrol routes and landmarks differ. Shared samples keep physical and rendered routes aligned. They reuse the industrial kit and relay/core/extraction mission rules. Solid trees stay visible on every preset; fuller tilted needle fans reuse retained CC0 fir photographs within a 5k triangle budget. Predictive tile preparation and worker terrain generation reduce visible seams and main-thread work, while the underlay remains until detailed ground is ready.

Each theater has two marked steel service facades and supported roofs. Forward roofs provide an optional SKY UPLINK, Anchor guardian, climbing Scout and two breakable cargo banks. Painted approach markings and the route map make entrances discoverable. One retaining wall per theater also shows damage, sparks and bounded rubble. Roof fields clip to supported surfaces, and debris can fall over the edge. The uplink EMP uses restrained actual-hit arcs and persistent enemy disruption markers.

Higgsfield supplies key art, menu video, armor albedo, three theater illustrations, two terrain albedos and three supply faceplates. Repair is a mint hexagon, energy a cyan tall cell, and coolant an amber compact canister; silhouettes and iconography support color-independent recognition. Generated illustrations are labeled concept art. The small faceplates are real runtime materials, not generated 3D meshes. Complete matched normal/roughness sets and more detailed architecture remain production work. Job IDs, prompts, hashes and normalization receipts remain in assets/reference/higgsfield-*.json.

The HUD keeps the aiming center clear. The route map shows objectives, both service routes, optional uplink, checkpoint and available supplies. Co-op displays squad positions and shared respawn progress; rival modes retain their information limits. Supply guidance describes E, blocked and full states, and actual claims give brief feedback. Reduced flashes restrain combat/weather pulses.
