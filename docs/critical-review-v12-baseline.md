The actual shipping floor audit covers 1,728 mixed hind feet with 4.000008 mm minimum corner clearance, 0.121 µm segment error and bounded casts. That fixture does not establish arbitrary terrain safety. The angular armor and material separation are a visible incremental improvement; title-art fidelity remains unfinished.

I viewed the optimized medium and close rooftop captures: fitted housing, fins and grilles are readable, with no visible clipping or broken PBR maps.
