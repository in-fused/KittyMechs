## Practical limits

This remains a playable prototype. Authored turning improves one part of feline motion. Start/brake bracing, supported landing, upright turns, steep/uneven terrain contact, cinematic rigs/materials and richer architecture still need work; see the feline motion brief. Strict support is bounded by fixed-surface footprint, grade and reach. Arbitrary rocks, ledges and dynamic platforms remain unqualified.

Authority heading can still reverse rapidly. Guest presentation interpolates snapshot state, so exact takeoff/landing acceptance needs diagnostics with the same displayed time and grounded/form state as contact. A latest packet's airborne flag alone is not reliable evidence of what the player currently sees.

Climbing supports marked workshop service facades and complete roof decks per theater, with Scout pursuit and optional guarded uplinks. General building access, indoor missions and structural fracture remain future work. Admitted walls/cargo have replicated matching collider/model retirement. Co-op checkpoints, supplies and current PvP/PvPvE rules remain playable.

Poor-network prediction, remote household NAT/TURN, eight-player stress, physical mobile hardware, worst-case combat and long sessions are not qualified. Durable hosting remains deferred; the friends service needs this PC, arena and tunnel alive. Weather changes visuals, lighting and fog rather than traction, perception or damage.

Original Unreal material stays read only; its deletion condition remains unmet. Normal builds/play do not read it. Canonical/editable source art is backed up on local-assets-snapshot before publication. The ongoing improvement goal stays active.
