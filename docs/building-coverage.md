This finite shipping inventory excludes authored props rejected by navigation admission and decorative foliage/rocks/lamps. The landmark kits render and collide from 96 shared boxes: Rainline42, Ember32 and Frostline22. Six cargo objects are small roof fixtures, rather than additional full-sized ground buildings.

The draft registers 36 city/workshop buildings. Workshops now use decoded shipping triangles on host and guest; city surfaces identify current surviving block IDs. All four sides are candidates. Actual footprint contact, full hull clearance, supported roof destinations, energy, death and destruction must still pass for every admitted journey. Narrow strips, a successful east wall, a metadata rectangle or a gross mesh bounding box cannot satisfy universal traversal.

The public V16 collision gaps include retaining walls with 6.3m visible geometry but 2m collision and cranes with 22.73m posts/beams and no collider. The next private source iteration replaces proxy collision for cargo, towers, pipes, retaining walls and cranes with 16,160 decoded shipping triangles. Authority and guest use the same actual prop origin, scale/yaw/translation once, one collider per prop, and stable current mesh ownership; real tower/crane cavities and pipe gaps remain empty. This covers the 16 standalone props and six rooftop cargo fixtures in the census, not new climb admission. Landmark boxes already share render/collision geometry, but still need stable IDs, exterior contact ownership, reachable ledges and support dependencies.

The critic independently approved the five-kind extraction as geometry only. Its 169 components are material-primitive edge-connected triangle sets, not automatically closed solids, load-bearing damage parts or fracture units. Some inherited components are open/non-manifold; an open crane component has negative signed volume. Candidate wall/upward patch classification is not universal outward-normal, claw support or roof admission. Actual local claw grip, airborne/pounce acquisition, landings and transitions remain to be implemented and qualified for these classes.

Destruction remains partial. The current123banks/952cells belong to22Rainline city buildings; six city buildings have no damage banks. Workshops and tower/landmark/crane classes remain immutable, and Ember/Frostline have no structural banks. Public breakable retaining walls and roof cargo remove whole cover proxies; the private source instead retires the exact whole mesh and its owned contacts. Neither produces local fracture or load-bearing collapse. Existing visual fragments do not establish collision-bearing rubble or whole-building destruction.

Each accepted removal must atomically update visible geometry, collision, paw contacts, roof support, navigation and replicated state. Damage location/direction should drive explicit component fracture and support failure; falling actors and bounded resting rubble need real collision and lifecycle ownership. Late join, unload/reentry and map transitions must reproduce the same state. These requirements apply to every theater and building class.

Owner clarification requires forceful pounce/landing impacts to damage the actual contacted material and moving claws to chip, degrade and loosen pieces during ascent. A stationary hold must not create repeated per-tick damage. All 84 shipping claws now have independently reviewed decoded topology and opt-in posed cap data; the existing full-paw solver remains active. Actual local damage, claw-cap contact activation, common rigid correction and whole-body/swept clearance are still open. Feline pounce, tackle and dive interactions against mechs/enemies likewise require actual authoritative combat and chassis-specific motion, rather than reference films or particles.

Population work must expand distinct connected districts and cooperative mission routes in all three maps, preserving collision readiness, route clearance and measured moving frame pacing at the supported player count. Current counts alone do not establish extensive population.

Evidence for this ledger is retained privately in `.sites-runtime/universal-climbing/structure-census/census-001.json`, `census-001.md` and `model-bounds-001.json`, including source/model hashes and admitted transforms. This ledger is a work record; release qualification remains separate.
