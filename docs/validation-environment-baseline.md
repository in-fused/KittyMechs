## Practical limits

This remains a playable prototype. Four chassis differ in silhouette, cadence and abilities; cinematic production rigs/materials, authored turning/landing transitions and richer map-wide art still need work. Actual fixed-surface contacts are bounded by footprint, grade and reach; dynamic platforms and arbitrary uneven edges remain unqualified. [The feline motion brief](feline-motion-direction.md) records the next direction.

Climbing supports two marked workshop service facades and complete roof decks per theater, with actual Scout pursuit and optional guarded uplinks. General building access, indoor missions and structural fracture are future work. Destruction covers admitted walls/cargo with matching replicated collider/model retirement.

Multiplayer and temporary HTTPS/WSS routes are exercised; remote household NAT/TURN, poor-network input prediction, eight-player stress, physical mobile hardware and long sessions are not qualified. Durable hosting remains deferred. The friends service needs this PC, arena and tunnel alive. Weather changes visuals, lighting and fog, not traction, perception or damage.

Original Unreal material stays read only and its deletion condition remains unmet. Normal builds/play do not read it. Shipping assets and provenance live in KittyMech-Web; ignored canonical/editable art is backed up on `local-assets-snapshot` before publication.
