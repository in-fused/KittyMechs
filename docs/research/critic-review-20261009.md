Approval applies only to the research and proposed production direction in `overhaul-20261009.md`, `subscription-free-assets-20261009.md`, and `evidence-20261009.json`. It does not resume the paused goal, authorize tool installation, admit an engine migration, approve implementation or release, or certify AAA quality.

The proposal addresses the requested four distinct feline mechs, quadruped/upright movement and conversion, coordinated attacker/victim combat, geometry-based climbing, three populated environments, persistent structural destruction, and cooperative progression. The documents retain that distinction and exclude training the replacement models on the bank.

Focused local reads verified that `shared/simulation.ts` gates the strike on airborne state, while also retaining a separate grounded boost/ability path; the blueprint's airborne-only characterization concerns the strike carrier. `felineStrikeMotion.ts` zeroes torso/head overlays for non-Caracal chassis. `cityRubble.ts` specifies 24 bodies and eight-second lifetimes. `package.json` supports the stated Three/R3F/Rapier, postprocessing, networking and optimization foundation. These findings substantiate the selective rebuild recommendation; they do not prove how the current candidate feels or what reached the public game.

The renderer/example evidence is appropriately limited: TitanCraft/Tokyo are not presented as proof of WebGPU, transferable licenses, matching multiplayer requirements or comparable production cost. Strata identity/access uncertainty, the inaccessible source repository and Unreal-related SimplyStream route remain disclosed. ShatterVox is excluded from commercial code reuse. No unsupported engine superiority or model superiority conclusion is required by the plan.

Nonblocking follow-ups before implementation:

- Select the target PC/browser/network and structural representation budgets before binding the proposed frame-time targets. Require ordinary gameplay, two-client action/destruction synchronization and persistent-ruin evidence before expansion.
- Preserve a per-file rights/source ledger and independent source backup before cleanup. The asset counts and snapshot branch are inventory evidence, not a new hash audit or off-PC recovery proof.

Review scope: one static pass, focused reads of four local implementation files, and one primary-source web batch covering Higgsfield terms, ComfyUI and TripoSR. External claims outside that batch were assessed for qualification and consistency against the supplied evidence register, not independently re-audited. No runtime, installation, generation, account, browser interaction, release or goal operation was performed.
