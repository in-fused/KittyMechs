# Initial deployment-menu observation

Staged only: `Tools/RuntimeTests/KMRuntimeDeployMenuTests.cpp.txt`, `deploy-menu-launcher.patch`, and the expanded parser-checked `Test-Runtime.DeployMenu.ps1.txt`. These have not been promoted, compiled or executed. The test depends on the existing runtime test file's public namespace helper functions.

`KittyMech.Runtime.DeployMenu.Safety` opens the real RainlineReach map only after the existing physical-directory, explicit UserDir, expected-root and fresh-save-slot checks. It starts standalone offline PIE with normal initial menu, player, checkpoint and enemies. It performs no input, placement, damage, enemy removal/spawn or menu operation. The initial baseline must appear before two simulation seconds, with full health, damage serial zero, first checkpoint, open menu and original live AI. This prevents accepting a late baseline after missed damage/recovery.

The observer watches 60 actual world simulation seconds, bounded by 90 wall seconds after readiness and a separate 15-second readiness limit. Every world callback checks health, damage serial, down/retry state, checkpoint-restored notifications, menu presence, player identity/position and original enemy population. It fails at the first violation and retains a final failure sample. Pawn state plus enemy positions, distance, obstruction-based line of sight, windup and health are sampled approximately ten times per simulation second. All original AI, timers and collision remain active.

The staged runner addition requires exact fresh automation and receipt results, unchanged health/damage/menu/checkpoint observations, zero fixture mutations, matching enemy identities and sample counts, and the complete bounded interval. A pass would establish only bounded initial-station survival with this map/seed. It would not establish general invulnerability, reopened-menu safety elsewhere, human input, rendering, networking or performance. A failure provides evidence before any protection policy is chosen.
