## Finding: pose blending destroys the contact constraint

The single root design flaw is using independently blended joint rotations as the final result of a world-space contact solve, while retaining `stance` and its support lease. `movingRFfeet067.ts:63` constructs the exact IK pose, then line65 replaces every chain joint with `baseline.slerp(solved, weight)`. The nonlinear forward-kinematic endpoint of those interpolated rotations is not the interpolated endpoint, much less the fixed support anchor. `solve` returns true for the pre-blend reach/solve, without checking the final endpoint. Both ordinary and R paths declare stance from their phase/target lifecycle rather than completion of the contact constraint.

## Measurement provenance: three different quantities

1. **Source geometry-derived marker definition.** At construction, the owner scans actual foot-family weighted vertices once, selects the lowest band, averages it, and maps that point into the paw's local frame as `soleLocal`. This is a source-derived marker, not an authored semantic plantar patch. The band includes descendant toe/claw skin and is converted into one rigid paw-relative point.
2. **Saved post-solve marker error.** `marker(f)` uses `paw.localToWorld(soleLocal)`. Diagnostics line140 evaluates it after all IK and source→IK blends. Thus the .41609/.63084 numbers are rendered-pose rigid-marker-to-lease distances. They are NOT pre-correction source-pose errors. Calling them merely source-marker errors obscures that the recorded pose is already the final blended pose. The raw evidence has no separate pre-IK marker or exact full-IK residual, so neither can be numerically reconstructed from these rows alone.
3. **Actual rendered skinned-sole/pad error.** No per-frame selected skin vertex/contact-patch witness is present. Descendant toes/claws can articulate relative to the paw; one cached paw-local average is not their current lowest surface. Therefore neither these errors nor near-zero rows prove the rendered pad is planted or clear. Actual pad penetration/slip and native contact remain unmeasured here. ## One bounded redesign candidate: contact as an endpoint constraint

Keep the existing single owner, restore-before-mixer discipline, measured signed gait phase, authored gait swing and transform core. Change only the contact task and lifecycle. The final correction is a solve of the final world-space foot task, not a post-solve blend of each joint.

- Explicit phases are source swing/unloaded, touch/release transfer, and planted. A hit/lease reserves a support candidate; only completed endpoint contact enters planted. A valid lease alone never labels a blended/acquiring/unloading foot as planted.
- For touch/release transfer, construct one continuous world-space effector trajectory between the current rendered endpoint and the source endpoint/support patch. Blend or ease that target in task space, maintaining clearance against the support plane and using the current source knee/hock pole and paw twist. Solve that final target at full constraint authority each frame. If no feasible continuous transfer exists, retain source swing or explicit unplanted release; do not snap, stretch a chain, lower tolerances or claim stance.
- During planted stance, the foot's selected plantar contact task stays at the leased world patch and is solved exactly. Source hip/core motion, limb translations/binds and source knee/hock pole remain inputs; source lift/swing owns release. A speed-band transition does not discard a reachable existing plant solely because the clip name changes: remap the source phase/footfall handoff while retaining its world anchor until the new source lift or a feasible planned release. This avoids repeated arbitrary correction ramps on a standing target.
- Moving-R fore unload is an explicit release transition into the actual authored front lift, never an increasingly source-dominant pose still called planted. Rear support transfers and fore replant use the same endpoint lifecycle. Preserve authored transform core/fore motion and measured carrier travel; do not freeze the whole transform with a full-body correction or create additive recoil/action substitutes. If source/native travel makes a plant unreachable, release visibly and replan a reachable footfall; never move the native carrier or silently drag its anchor.
- Feed the task with a stable plantar patch supported by the parent-owned bounded actual skin witnesses. Evaluate only the cached small selected set with actual skin weights and bind transforms. Keep rigid marker diagnostics explicitly separate; log pre-source endpoint, commanded task, post-solve endpoint and actual selected-patch support residual/provenance. A patch must account for its articulated digits/normal; a paw-relative averaged lowest point cannot certify it. No all-vertex runtime scan or heavy logging is needed.

This candidate addresses one mechanism, rather than installing another footfall owner: authored phase chooses lift, native authority moves the carrier, one contact task determines the constrained limb endpoint, and one final solve produces the displayed limb. Supported combat/climb/pair fallback contracts remain unchanged; those actions do not acquire new contact claims. It is a presentation constraint design, not a force/balance certificate.

## Bounded acceptance and remaining uncertainties

Before implementation, independently review the task-space design and parent diagnostic capability. Then use one integrated ordinary-public-input capture of clear forward/reverse quadruped/upright travel, acceleration through existing gait bands, deceleration/resume and moving R both ways. The useful observation must separate source swing, transferring contact and actually planted patch, with synchronized owner provenance. Judge visible joints/lift, floor clearance, fixed planted patches and fluid R; tests alone cannot accept it. A superficially smaller scalar error or relabeled rows are insufficient.

Unknowns remain: exact preblend source and full-IK errors, articulated pad envelope, knee/hock feasibility under the retained core transform, full stance-to-swing continuity, true lateral travel and the user's actual browser cost. This diagnosis cannot promise a complete natural feline gait. It makes no camera/culling/native-physics changes and does not reinterpret the LIMITED047 verdict.
