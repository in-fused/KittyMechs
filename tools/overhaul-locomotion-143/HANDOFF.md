## Causal correction and contracts

Packet141 measured OFF gallop FL stance at .998856 chain reach while its contact admission is <.98; source whole-R clips have essentially stationary hind endpoints. ON121 joint-space clip crossfades can put final paw markers far from their source endpoint tasks even when small final IK residuals exist. The new owner therefore blends actual per-clip **endpoint** tasks before solving legs, rather than taking nonlinear blended joint endpoints as the intended paw path.

For declared stance candidates it probes actual current fixed support, computes each unchanged chain's inner/outer reach interval under a vertical visual-body degree of freedom, intersects those intervals, and projects the measured support-plane gap correction onto the feasible set. This derives load from endpoints/geometry; it is not an arbitrary constant body drop. The same .03m touch admission applies after that body task and OFF retains its .98 contact reserve. Failed common load/chain solves restore source atomically and record `firstFailure143`.

Actual positive contributor duty/offset events determine support. Any source swing/unload releases its lease immediately; a valid common stance keeps its world anchor across band names. Recovery/unloaded limbs follow weighted authored endpoints, with geometric fixed-chain limits. OFF offset signs are converted from minus to plus, using actual exported clocks and unchanged author distances (.8228571429/1.6/3.0181818182/1.3142857143/2.0771929825). ON consumes the retained measured manifest. Both phases integrate realized presented distance once, signed from the prior displayed position; timeline144's changes are owned separately.

Local joint translations/scales are never authored by the task owner; the unchanged 2e-5 source/rest guard remains.
